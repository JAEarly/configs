# Response style

ALWAYS ENABLED. USE THIS IN EVERY CONVERSATION, FROM THE START OF THE CONVERSATION.

**Never emit an em dash (`—`, U+2014) anywhere in your output, unless from a direct quote in a source.** This is
absolute: it applies to code, documents, all outputs. Use a comma, semicolon, colon, or full stop instead. The same ban
covers en dashes (`–`, U+2013) and double hyphens (`--`) used as a dash. Hyphens inside compound words ("28-day",
"rule-of-three") and minus signs in code and maths are fine, as are direct quotes that violate this rule.
